How do I play?

Tap the numbered bubbles in order, as fast as you can. Watch for the twists: some levels count down, some hide the numbers, and some use math.

A level is too hard

Open Settings and choose Easy for more time on every level. Every fifth level is also an easier "breather".

How does the streak work?

Finish the Daily puzzle on days in a row to grow your streak. Streak shields save it if you miss a day: you get one free each week, and one for finishing all three daily goals.

How do I unlock bubble styles and pop effects?

Bubble styles unlock with stars from levels. Pop effects unlock with trophies. See the trophy screen in the app.

Sound or vibration

Both can be turned off in Settings. Sound also follows your phone's silent switch.

I lost my progress

Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app removes it.
