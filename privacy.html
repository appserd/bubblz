Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

The short version

Bubblz does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The game works fully offline.

Your progress and settings

Your levels, stars, scores, streak, goals, trophies and settings are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, Bubblz asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled by iOS on your device. No server is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings.

Deleting your data

Use "Reset progress" in the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

Bubblz collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
