bubblz

Pop numbers. Train your brain.

Bubblz is a brain game for every age. Tap the numbered bubbles in order, as fast as you can. Easy to learn in seconds, and it keeps getting smarter the more you play.

For young learners: counting, number order and simple math, built into the game.

For grown-ups and seniors: short, calm rounds that keep your mind active, with large clear numbers and an Easy mode.

For speed lovers: Rush mode, combos and best scores to beat.

Endless levels with new twists, a Daily puzzle, daily goals and trophies. No ads, no accounts, no internet needed.
